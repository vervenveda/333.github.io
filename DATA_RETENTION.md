# 333 Network — Public Data Retention Information

333 Network includes local-first workspaces. Browser-local records may remain on the device until they are deleted by the user, removed by an application, or cleared by browser/device storage management. Private browsing modes and device changes may affect persistence.

Where available, use in-app export and deletion controls to manage your work. An export should be retained somewhere you control before clearing local storage.

A locally saved profile or project is not evidence that an authenticated, synchronized service or hosted account exists. Retention policies for connected services must be disclosed in their applicable user notices when activated.

For privacy questions concerning a live service, contact the project owner privately. Never publish personal records, credentials, or recovery information in a public issue.

This document does not publish operational logs, storage architecture, deletion procedures, disaster-recovery plans, or internal security retention schedules.
