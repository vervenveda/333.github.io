Network Principles
Connection should remain understandable and voluntary.

        

          
Connection by Choice
Members decide whether to answer, join, activate media, appear in a directory, publish work, or participate in a community feed.

          
Shared Identity, Separate Control
HOLLO can supply a local identity while each application keeps its own purpose, permissions, records, and export controls.

          
Honest Infrastructure
Local browser tools are not represented as live carrier, hosted meeting, mail-delivery, publishing, or synchronized cloud services until those systems exist.
