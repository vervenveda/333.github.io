# 333 Network — Community Code of Conduct

333 Network supports respectful communication, creative collaboration, and accessible participation. Everyone participating through project discussions, feedback, and community spaces should be treated with dignity.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experiences, abilities, and perspectives.
- Disagree with ideas without harassing people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries.
- Give credit for others' work and honor copyright.
- Avoid spam, impersonation, threats, discriminatory language, and unwanted contact.
- Do not pressure anyone, particularly minors, into private communication.
- Report safety and security concerns privately where appropriate.

## Scope and reporting

This code covers official 333 Network project discussion and contributions. Concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainers through an appropriate established contact channel. Do not publish private identifying information, credentials, or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may moderate content and participation to protect the community. Reports will be handled with reasonable care; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when safety or legal obligations require action.
