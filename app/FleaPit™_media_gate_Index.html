How FleaPit Works

  • FleaPit is a free-media guide and personal library, not a movie-hosting service.
  • The hub begins disconnected. Choose Connect to Watch before opening an external source.
  • Use Movies, Documentaries, Family, Learning, Archives, and other categories to narrow the library.
  • Family Controls can hide Teen, Mature, or Unrated items behind a local PIN.
  • Local video and audio files remain in memory for the current tab and are not uploaded.
  • Export your saved library regularly because browser storage can be cleared.
