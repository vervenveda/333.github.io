# 333 Network — Public Privacy Notice

**Last revised:** November 7, 2026

333 Network is a local-first collection of communication, community, creative, and planning workspaces. Some features remain in development. This notice describes the publicly available experience rather than unlaunched services.

## Information you choose to provide

You may enter profile details, meeting plans, drafts, project content, creative posts, or other information into a workspace. Some tools store information in your browser or device. Information you choose to publish or share through an available feature may become visible to its intended audience.

A browser-local profile is not a verified membership or authenticated hosted account. Drafts and local records are not automatically synchronized across devices. Clearing browser data, changing devices, or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored material. Export important work when an export option is available.

## Your choices

Use camera, microphone, sharing, and community participation features only when you choose to do so and when supported by your browser. Review a workspace's interface before making information public. Do not enter passwords, private keys, or confidential records into public feedback forms or repository discussions.

## Service availability and third parties

The public interface identifies browser-local features separately from connected services that are not yet operational. Some pages may link to third-party services or websites, which have their own practices and policies. A future connected service will need an appropriate, updated notice before being publicly enabled.

## Children and families

A general network profile is not a substitute for age-appropriate, consent-based child services. Children should use only the experiences designated for their age and with appropriate guardian guidance.

## Access, exports, and requests

Where a workspace provides export or local deletion controls, you can use them to manage your device-stored information. Local browser data may also be cleared through your browser's settings. For questions about information processed by an enabled connected service, contact the project owner through an appropriate private channel. Do not include sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

## Retention, updates, and security

Browser-local records generally remain until you delete them, the application removes them, or browser/device storage changes. Any retention period for a future hosted service must be communicated when that service launches. Security concerns should be reported privately. This notice will be revised as actual capabilities change.
