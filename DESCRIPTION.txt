# Overview

The **333™ Network** is the communications and digital infrastructure platform of the **Verve N Veda™** ecosystem.

Rather than serving as a traditional social network, 333 is designed as a sovereign communications environment where individuals, families, educators, artists, organizations, businesses, and communities can safely connect through a unified collection of applications.

The platform is built with:

- Vanilla HTML
- Vanilla CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Progressive Web App technology
- GitHub Pages
- Local-first architecture
- Future cloud synchronization

The guiding philosophy is simple:

> **Own your data. Own your identity. Own your digital home.**
